Dragon Quest IX Modern v4: a launcher, an HD title screen in your language, three save slots and many menus redone.

**New since v3.2**
- **The launcher**: news, updates, the HD pack, options and keys in one window; PLAY starts the game once everything is ready.
- **HD title screen**: the title's picture in HD (with the HD pack), its logo in the game's language; the quick save's questions and the Wi-Fi settings in our windows.
- **Three save slots**: one adventure alone, another with friends. On the title screen L changes the adventure and X names it; also Game > Saves.
- **Items > Use several**: a "Use several" row under Use, for seeds and healing herbs (European version).
- **Equip**: its keys' bar and picture sharp again, with your own keys.
- **The party's HP and MP**: the large panel only with the X menu.
- **Lists**: Patty's inn and a companion's bag shown whole, clear of the dialogue.
- **HD textures**: the same picture in other colours takes its HD texture too.
- **Townspeople in 3D**: those behind a counter face the right way.

**HD pack 3**: the remade opening movie and the HD title's pictures. The game downloads the whole pack again once (about 1.8 GB), in the background as before.

**Already installed?** Open the launcher (or the game): it updates itself, your saves and settings are kept.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you update to v4.
